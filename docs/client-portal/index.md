# Client Portal Overview

## Quick Reference

The Client Portal is where your clients view and interact with everything you send them - quotes, contracts, invoices, questionnaires, and galleries. It's their personalized hub for their photography experience with you.

**What Clients Can Do in the Portal:**
- View and accept quotes with package details
- Review and sign contracts digitally
- Pay invoices securely via Stripe
- View and download their photo galleries
- Fill out questionnaires
- Track their booking status and payment history

**How Access Works:**
- No login required - clients receive magic links via email
- Each link is unique and secure (portal tokens)
- Links work on any device (mobile, tablet, desktop)
- All information is private and secure

**Why It Matters:**
The portal creates a professional, seamless experience for your clients. Instead of emailing PDFs back and forth or using multiple platforms, everything happens in one branded location. This makes you look organized and makes their life easier - a win-win!

**Next Steps:** Learn about [portal access](./portal-access), [quote acceptance](./quote-acceptance), or [contract signing](./contract-signing).

## Detailed Guide

### What is the Client Portal?

Think of the Client Portal as your client's personal dashboard for their photography project. When you send a quote, contract, invoice, or gallery, your client receives an email with a secure link that takes them to their portal page.

**The portal is where clients:**
- See the status of their booking (quote pending, contract signed, payment complete)
- Review your professional proposals and packages
- Complete required steps (signing, payment, questionnaires)
- Access their delivered photos
- Download invoices and signed contracts

**What makes it special:**
- **No account needed** - Clients don't create passwords or remember logins
- **Mobile-friendly** - Works perfectly on phones and tablets
- **Branded** - Shows your business name, logo, and professional styling
- **Secure** - Each link is unique and only accessible to that specific client
- **All-in-one** - Everything they need is in one place

### The Client Experience

Let's walk through what your client sees when they interact with the portal:

#### Receiving the Email

When you send a quote, your client receives an email from your ShootPath account. The email includes:
- Your business name in the sender line
- A friendly message about what you're sending ("Your quote is ready!")
- A prominent button or link: "View Quote"
- Context about what to do next

The email is professional, clear, and doesn't overwhelm them with technical jargon.

#### Clicking the Link

When they click "View Quote" (or any portal link), they're taken to a clean, branded page showing:
- Your business logo at the top
- Their name and what they're booking
- The specific content (quote, contract, invoice, etc.)
- Clear action buttons for what to do next

Everything is designed to be simple and intuitive. Your client shouldn't need instructions to know what to do!

#### Taking Action

Depending on what you sent, they can:
- **Review a quote** and click "Accept" or "Decline"
- **Read a contract** and add their digital signature
- **Pay an invoice** using credit card, debit card, or bank transfer
- **Answer questions** in a questionnaire
- **Browse their gallery** and select favorites or purchase prints

Each action is straightforward, with clear buttons and confirmation messages.

#### Seeing Their Status

The portal shows clients where they are in the process:
- Quote pending → "Waiting for your decision"
- Quote accepted → "Next: Sign your contract"
- Contract signed → "Next: Submit your deposit payment"
- Payment complete → "You're all booked! We'll send session details soon"

This transparency reduces anxiety and questions. Clients always know what's happening and what comes next.

### Portal Security and Privacy

Your clients' information is protected in several ways:

#### Portal Tokens

Every client gets a unique portal token - a long, random string of characters that's part of their link. This token acts like a secure key:
- Only someone with the exact link can access the portal page
- Tokens are impossible to guess
- Each client has their own unique token
- Tokens don't reveal any information about the client or booking

**Example portal link:**
```
https://yourshootpath.com/portal/abc123xyz789/quote
```

The `abc123xyz789` part is the unique token. Without it, nobody can access that client's information.

#### No Shared Access

Unlike traditional logins where someone might guess a password, portal links are:
- Not reusable across clients (each link is unique)
- Not searchable (you can't browse to find others' portals)
- Not discoverable (there's no directory of portal pages)

If someone doesn't have the exact link, they can't see anything.

#### Email Delivery

Portal links are sent directly to the email address you specify for that client. As long as your client's email account is secure, their portal access is secure.

:::tip Best Practice
Always double-check the email address before sending quotes or contracts! A typo means the link goes to the wrong person.
:::

#### HTTPS Encryption

All portal pages use HTTPS encryption (the padlock in the browser). This means:
- Data is encrypted in transit
- Nobody can intercept and read the information
- Payments are PCI-compliant and secure
- Industry-standard security protections apply

### What Clients See vs. What You See

It's helpful to understand the difference between your admin view and the client's portal view:

#### Admin View (What You See)

When you log into ShootPath, you see:
- All clients, leads, and jobs
- Backend controls and settings
- Ability to create, edit, and delete anything
- Financial reports and business analytics
- Team member activity (if applicable)

#### Portal View (What Clients See)

Your client's portal page shows ONLY:
- Their own information
- Content you've sent to them (quotes, contracts, invoices)
- Actions they need to take
- Their galleries and photos

Clients can't see:
- Other clients' information
- Your backend settings or controls
- Other jobs or bookings
- Your financial information
- Anything you haven't explicitly sent to them

This keeps things simple for them and protects everyone's privacy.

### Common Client Portal Workflows

Here are the typical journeys your clients take through the portal:

#### Booking Workflow (Quote → Contract → Payment)

1. Client receives email: "Your quote is ready"
2. Clicks link → Reviews packages and pricing in portal
3. Clicks "Accept Quote" → ShootPath creates job and contract
4. Receives new email: "Your contract is ready to sign"
5. Clicks link → Reviews contract in portal
6. Signs contract digitally → Contract is locked in
7. Receives new email: "Your invoice is ready"
8. Clicks link → Pays deposit via Stripe in portal
9. Sees confirmation: "You're all booked!"

**All of this happens seamlessly** without the client needing to juggle multiple platforms or send documents back and forth.

#### Gallery Delivery Workflow

1. You upload photos and mark gallery as ready
2. Client receives email: "Your gallery is ready!"
3. Clicks link → Browses photos in portal
4. Can favorite photos, download selections, or share with family
5. Optionally purchases prints or digital downloads if you offer them

#### Questionnaire Workflow

1. You send a questionnaire (like a wedding timeline worksheet)
2. Client receives email with portal link
3. Fills out questions in portal
4. Submits responses
5. You receive notification and can review their answers

Each workflow is designed to minimize friction and maximize professionalism.

### Mobile Experience

Most of your clients will access the portal from their phones - especially for quick tasks like reviewing quotes or signing contracts. The portal is fully responsive and mobile-optimized:

**What works great on mobile:**
- Viewing quotes and package details
- Signing contracts (touch signature works perfectly)
- Paying invoices via Apple Pay, Google Pay, or saved cards
- Browsing galleries and favoriting photos
- Filling out simple questionnaires

**What's better on desktop:**
- Reviewing long contracts with lots of terms
- Downloading high-resolution gallery photos
- Purchasing multiple prints or products
- Filling out lengthy questionnaires

The portal automatically adapts to whatever device your client is using, so they always get a good experience.

**Professional Styling** matches modern design standards - clean, readable, and trustworthy.

### Common Questions About the Portal

#### "Do clients need to create an account?"

Nope! The portal uses magic links - clients just click the link in their email and they're in. No passwords to remember or accounts to create.

#### "What if they lose the email with the link?"

You can resend any portal link from your admin dashboard. Just open the job and click "Resend Quote Email" or "Resend Contract Email" and they'll get a fresh link.

#### "Can they share the link with someone else?"

Technically yes - the link works for anyone who has it. But the content is specific to that booking, so there's no reason to share it. If they want to show someone (like their partner reviewing a quote), that's fine!

#### "Do portal links expire?"

Nope! Links remain active throughout the job lifecycle. A client can access their signed contract or gallery months later using the same link.

#### "Can I see what they've viewed?"

You can see when they accept a quote, sign a contract, or submit payment. ShootPath tracks the status of each step so you know where things stand.

#### "What if they have questions while viewing the portal?"

Portal pages include your contact information, and clients can simply reply to the email you sent. Most clients will just text or email you directly if they have questions!

### Tips for a Great Client Experience

**Send links promptly** - The faster you respond with a quote or contract link, the more professional you appear. Aim for same-day or next-day turnaround.

**Include context in emails** - When sending a portal link, add a personal note in the email: "Hey Sarah! Here's the pricing we discussed for your October wedding. Let me know if you have any questions!"

**Test your own portal** - Create a test lead and send yourself a quote to see exactly what your clients experience. This helps you spot any confusing wording or branding issues.

**Keep portal content clear** - When configuring contract templates, package descriptions, or email templates, write in plain language. Avoid legal jargon or overly technical terms.

**Follow up if needed** - If a client doesn't respond to a quote in 3-5 days, send a friendly follow-up email asking if they have questions. Sometimes they just forgot!

**Educate clients on next steps** - After they accept a quote, send a quick text or email: "Awesome! You should receive a contract to sign next - check your email!" This reduces confusion.
