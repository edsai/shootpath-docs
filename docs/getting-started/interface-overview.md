# Interface Overview

This guide introduces the main areas of the ShootPath interface to help you get started quickly.

## Dashboard

Your central hub for tracking business performance and upcoming work.

![Dashboard Overview](/img/screenshots/interface/dashboard-annotated.png)

**Key Areas:**

1. **Main Navigation** — Switch between Dashboard, Leads, Jobs, Calendar, Galleries, Clients, Reports, and Settings. Full-width navbar for better visibility.
2. **Quick Actions & Account** — Switch between studios (if you belong to multiple), check notifications, toggle dark/light theme, and access your account menu. The "+ New Lead" button is now in the dashboard page header.
3. **Key Metrics** — Track new leads, upcoming shoots, active leads, and jobs in production at a glance.
4. **Revenue Overview** — See your monthly and yearly revenue, average job value, and financial trends.
5. **Sessions Calendar** — View confirmed sessions and jobs that need follow-up for scheduling.

---

## Leads

Manage all client inquiries and send quotes.

![Leads Overview](/img/screenshots/interface/leads-annotated.png)

**Key Areas:**

1. **Quick Actions** — Create new leads from inquiries or add events to your calendar.
2. **Filter Leads** — Filter by status and job type to focus on specific inquiries.
3. **Leads Overview** — View all inquiries with client info, job type, status, quote details, and session dates.
4. **Lead Actions** — Send quotes, follow up, or convert accepted quotes into jobs.

---

## Jobs

Track all booked sessions through your workflow.

![Jobs Overview](/img/screenshots/interface/jobs-annotated.png)

**Key Areas:**

1. **Search & Filter** — Search jobs by name or filter by active, completed, or payment status.
2. **Jobs List** — All booked jobs with job number, date, client, type, status, and workflow progress.
3. **Workflow Progress** — Track where each job is in your workflow: last completed step and next step needed.
4. **Quick Actions** — View job details, contracts, invoices, or photo galleries.

---

## Calendar

Schedule and view all your photography sessions.

![Calendar Overview](/img/screenshots/interface/calendar-annotated.png)

**Key Areas:**

1. **Month Navigation** — Navigate between months to view your schedule. Today is highlighted.
2. **View Options** — Switch between Month, Week, and Day views.
3. **Job Type Filters** — Filter calendar by job type to focus on specific sessions.
4. **Calendar Grid** — View all scheduled sessions by date. Click dates to schedule.
5. **Session Events** — Click events to view details or make changes.

---

## Settings

Configure your business details, branding, and integrations.

![Settings Overview](/img/screenshots/interface/settings-annotated.png)

**Key Areas:**

1. **Settings Categories** — Navigate between different settings sections using these tabs.
2. **Business Settings** — Configure your business name, logo, contact information, and branding.
3. **Settings Cards** — Each card represents a configurable aspect of your business. Click to edit.
4. **Quick Edit** — Click any card to edit settings. Changes save automatically.
