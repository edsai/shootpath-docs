# Plans and Pricing

## Quick Reference

ShootPath offers three subscription tiers designed to grow with your photography business. Every plan includes the core CRM features you need to manage clients, send quotes, sign contracts, collect payments, and deliver galleries. Higher tiers add team collaboration, advanced automation, and priority support.

**Our Plans:**
- **Starter** - $29/month - Solo photographers and small studios
- **Professional** - $59/month - Growing businesses with advanced needs
- **Studio** - $99/month - Established studios with team members

**Save 20% with Annual Billing:**
- Starter: $279/year (save $69)
- Professional: $569/year (save $139)
- Studio: $959/year (save $229)

**All Plans Include:**
- 14-day free trial (no credit card required)
- Unlimited clients, leads, and jobs
- Quote and contract management
- Invoicing and payment tracking
- Client portal
- Mobile app access
- Email support

:::tip Start Your Free Trial
Not sure which plan is right? Start with our 14-day free trial of the Professional plan. You'll have access to all features so you can thoroughly test ShootPath before committing.

### Choosing the Right Plan

Not sure which plan fits your needs? Here's how to decide:

**Choose Starter if:**
- You're a solo photographer (no team members)
- You're just starting your business or work part-time
- You have fewer than 20 clients per year
- You don't need automation or workflows
- Your photo storage needs are under 10 GB
- You're comfortable with basic features

**Choose Professional if:**
- You're a full-time photographer
- You book 20-50+ clients per year
- You want to automate repetitive tasks
- You need advanced features like workflows and mini sessions
- You store 10-50 GB of photos
- You want faster email support

**Choose Studio if:**
- You have a team (photographers, assistants, admins)
- Multiple people need access to the system
- You need role-based permissions
- You want white-label branding for your gallery
- You store 50+ GB of photos
- You need API access or custom integrations
- You want priority support and onboarding help

**Still Not Sure?**
Start with the 14-day free trial of Professional. You'll have access to all features (including workflows, mini sessions, and 50 GB storage) so you can see what you actually use. You can always downgrade to Starter or upgrade to Studio based on what you learn during the trial.

### Upgrading Your Plan

As your business grows, you can upgrade to a higher plan at any time.

**How to Upgrade:**
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select the plan you want to upgrade to
4. Review the prorated charges
5. Click **Confirm Upgrade**

**Prorated Charges:**
When you upgrade mid-month:
- You're charged a prorated amount for the remaining days in the current billing period
- For example, if you upgrade from Starter ($29) to Professional ($59) with 15 days left in the month:
  - You'll be charged: (($59 - $29) / 30 days) × 15 days = $15
  - Your next full charge will be $59 on your renewal date

**What Happens Immediately:**
- All new features are available instantly
- Storage limits increase immediately
- You can start using workflows, advanced galleries, etc.
- Your billing date doesn't change

**Team Member Addition (Studio only):**
When you upgrade to Studio or add team members:
- First 5 users are included in the $99/month base price
- Additional users are $15/month each
- New users are prorated for the current billing period
- You can add/remove team members anytime

### Downgrading Your Plan

If you need to reduce costs or don't need certain features, you can downgrade to a lower plan.

**How to Downgrade:**
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Change Plan**
3. Select the plan you want to downgrade to
4. Review the limitations and what you'll lose
5. Click **Confirm Downgrade**

**What Happens:**
- Your downgrade takes effect at the end of your current billing period
- You keep access to all features until then
- You'll be charged the lower rate on your next billing date
- No refund for the current period (you already paid for the full month)

**Before Downgrading, Consider:**

**Starter → Losing Professional features:**
- Workflows stop running (they're paused, not deleted)
- Extra contract/email templates are hidden (not deleted)
- Storage over 10 GB remains accessible but you can't upload more
- Mini sessions won't accept new bookings

**Professional → Losing Studio features:**
- Team members lose access (except the owner)
- White-label gallery reverts to ShootPath branding
- API access is disabled
- Storage over 50 GB remains accessible but you can't upload more

**Downgrade Best Practice:**
Before downgrading:
1. Export any data or reports you need
2. Download photos if over the new storage limit
3. Inform team members if removing their access
4. Archive or simplify workflows if downgrading from Professional to Starter

You can always re-upgrade if you change your mind (your workflows, templates, and settings are preserved).

### Annual vs. Monthly Billing

You can choose to pay monthly or annually. Annual billing saves 20%.

**Monthly Billing:**
- **Starter:** $29/month
- **Professional:** $59/month
- **Studio:** $99/month

**Benefits:**
- Lower upfront cost
- Flexibility to cancel anytime
- Easy to upgrade/downgrade

**Annual Billing:**
- **Starter:** $279/year (save $69 = 20% off)
- **Professional:** $569/year (save $139 = 20% off)
- **Studio:** $959/year (save $229 = 20% off)

**Benefits:**
- Save 20% compared to monthly
- Lock in current pricing for a year
- One payment and forget about it
- Shows up as single expense for accounting

**Switching to Annual:**
You can switch to annual billing at any time:
1. Go to **Settings → Billing**
2. Click **Switch to Annual**
3. You'll be charged for a full year minus the prorated value of your current monthly period
4. Your billing date changes to the switch date

**Example:**
You're on Professional monthly ($59) and paid on the 1st of the month. On the 10th, you decide to switch to annual:
- You've used 10 days of your $59 monthly payment
- Remaining value: ($59 / 30) × 20 days = $39.33
- Annual cost: $569
- You're charged today: $569 - $39.33 = $529.67
- Next charge: $569 on the same date next year

**Annual Billing Refunds:**
If you cancel an annual plan:
- No refund for unused months (per our terms of service)
- Exception: If you cancel within 30 days of purchase and used less than 25% of features, we'll refund the prorated amount (contact billing support)
- Consider pausing instead of cancelling (see [Cancellation](./cancellation))

### Adding Team Members (Studio Plan)

The Studio plan includes 5 users. Add more for $15/month per user.

**How to Add a Team Member:**
1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Invite Team Member**
3. Enter their email address
4. Select their role:
   - **Admin:** Full access except billing
   - **Team Member:** Can manage assigned jobs, limited settings access
   - **Limited:** View-only access to assigned jobs
5. Click **Send Invite**

The team member receives an email with a link to create their account. Once they sign up, they're added to your team.

**Billing for Team Members:**
- Team members are prorated when added mid-month
- You're charged $15/month per team member (prorated) on your next billing date
- Charges continue until you remove the team member
- No charge for invited team members who haven't accepted yet

**Example:**
You're on Studio ($99/month, includes 5 users) and you already have 5 team members. You add a 6th team member on the 10th of the month:
- Prorated charge: ($15 / 30 days) × 20 days = $10
- Your next invoice will be: $99 (base) + $15 (6th member) = $114/month

**Removing Team Members:**
1. Go to **Settings → Team**
2. Click **Remove** next to the team member
3. Confirm removal

The team member immediately loses access. The charge for that team member stops at the end of the current billing period (no prorated refund).

- Delete old gallery photos after delivery (keep locally backed up)

### Payment Processing Fees

ShootPath's subscription fee covers the CRM software. Payment processing (when clients pay you) is handled by Stripe, which charges separately.

**Stripe's Standard Fees:**
- 2.9% + $0.30 per successful card charge
- Example: Client pays $1,000 invoice = $29.30 fee

**Who Pays the Fee:**
- You (the photographer) absorb the fee
- Client is charged exactly the invoice amount
- Stripe deducts the fee before depositing to your bank

**Stripe Pricing Varies by Volume:**
If you process over $100,000/year with Stripe, you may qualify for custom pricing (lower fees). Contact Stripe directly to negotiate.

**ShootPath's Role:**
- We don't mark up Stripe fees
- We don't take a percentage of your transactions
- You pay only your ShootPath subscription + Stripe's fee to Stripe

This is one of ShootPath's core advantages: flat subscription pricing with no transaction fees on our end.

## Common Questions

**Can I switch plans anytime?**
Yes! Upgrade anytime (effective immediately) or downgrade at the end of your current billing period. See [Upgrading](#upgrading-your-plan) and [Downgrading](#downgrading-your-plan) above.

**What happens if I exceed my storage limit?**
You can still download existing photos, but you can't upload new ones until you:
- Delete old photos to free up space
- Purchase a storage add-on
- Upgrade to a plan with more storage

**Do you offer custom plans for large studios?**
For studios with 10+ team members or 500+ GB storage needs, contact [sales@shootpath.com](mailto:sales@shootpath.com). We can create a custom plan with:
- Discounted per-user pricing
- Dedicated storage
- Priority support with dedicated rep
- Custom onboarding and training

**Can I change from monthly to annual billing?**
Yes. Go to **Settings → Billing → Switch to Annual**. You'll be charged for a full year minus the prorated value of your current monthly period (see [Annual vs. Monthly Billing](#annual-vs-monthly-billing) above).

**What's included in the free trial?**
The 14-day trial includes all Professional plan features:
- Unlimited clients, leads, jobs
- All templates and features
- 50 GB storage
- Workflows, mini sessions, etc.

You won't be charged unless you add a payment method and activate a subscription. See [Billing Overview](./index#free-trial) for details.

**Do I need to pay for Stripe separately?**
Yes. ShootPath integrates with Stripe for payment processing, but Stripe is a separate service:
- Create a free Stripe account (stripe.com)
- Connect it to ShootPath (Settings → Integrations → Stripe)
- Stripe charges 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction
- These fees go to Stripe, not ShootPath

**Can I try a higher plan before committing?**
Yes! During your trial, you have Professional features. To try Studio features (team members, white-label gallery):
1. Contact [sales@shootpath.com](mailto:sales@shootpath.com)
2. We'll temporarily upgrade your trial to Studio
3. Test team features during your trial
4. Choose the right plan when activating

**What if I only need ShootPath seasonally?**
Consider pausing your subscription during off-season:
1. Go to **Settings → Billing → Pause Subscription**
2. Choose pause duration (1-6 months)
3. No charges during the pause
4. Your account and data are preserved
5. Subscription resumes automatically after the pause

See [Cancellation](./cancellation#pausing-vs-cancelling) for details.
