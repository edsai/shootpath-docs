# Marketing Mini Sessions

## Quick Reference

Getting your mini session event to sell out requires strategic marketing across multiple channels. Start promoting 6-8 weeks in advance and create urgency as slots fill up.

**Marketing Channels:**
- Social media (Instagram, Facebook)
- Email marketing to past clients
- Local Facebook groups
- Your website
- Referrals and word-of-mouth
- Paid advertising (optional)

**Key Strategies:**
- Create urgency with limited spots
- Use early bird pricing
- Show compelling visual examples
- Make booking easy (direct link)
- Leverage social proof
- Follow up with interested leads

**Timeline:** Start marketing 6-8 weeks before event, with regular reminders as you approach the date.

**Next Steps:** [Create your event](./creating-events) or [optimize pricing](./pricing-strategies).

## Detailed Guide

### The Marketing Timeline

Successful mini session marketing follows a strategic timeline. Here's what to do when:

#### 6-8 Weeks Before Event

**Announcement Phase**

This is when you launch your event and create initial excitement.

**Actions:**
- Create event in ShootPath with public booking page
- Design announcement graphics for social media
- Write announcement email
- Plan your content calendar

**Social Media Post Example:**

> 🍂 FALL MINI SESSIONS ANNOUNCED! 🍂
>
> Mark your calendars! I'm offering limited Fall Mini Sessions on Saturday, October 14th at Oak Grove Park.
>
> ✨ 20-minute sessions
> ✨ 15 edited digital photos
> ✨ $250 per session
>
> EARLY BIRD SPECIAL: Book by September 15th and save $25 with code EARLYBIRD
>
> Only 12 spots available! Link in bio to book your time slot.
>
> Perfect for family photos, holiday cards, or updating your home gallery. Don't miss out!
>
> [Beautiful example photo from past fall session]

**Email Announcement:**

> Subject: 🍂 Fall Mini Sessions - Limited Spots Available!
>
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm excited to announce Fall Mini Sessions at Oak Grove Park!
>
> **When:** Saturday, October 14th
> **Where:** Oak Grove Park (gorgeous fall foliage!)
> **Session:** 20 minutes

> Book by September 15th and save $25 with code EARLYBIRD

#### 4-5 Weeks Before

**Momentum Phase**

Keep the event top-of-mind and show that spots are filling.

**Actions:**
- Share behind-the-scenes content (location scouting)
- Post sample photos from previous mini sessions
- Update followers on how many spots remain
- Share client testimonials from past events

**Social Media Post Example:**

> Can't wait for Fall Mini Sessions! 🍁

**Urgency Phase**

Create FOMO (fear of missing out) as slots get scarce.

**Actions:**
- Announce "half full" or "only X spots left"
- Share another visual example
- Remind followers of what's included
- Offer to answer any questions

#### 1 Week Before

**Last Call Phase**

Final push to fill remaining slots (if any) and build excitement for booked clients.

**Actions:**
- "Last chance" post if spots remain
- Thank everyone who booked
- Build excitement for event day
- Remind booked clients to prepare (what to wear, etc.)

**Social Media Post Example:**

### Social Media Marketing

Social media is your most powerful tool for mini session marketing. Here's how to use it effectively:

#### Instagram Strategy

**Content Types to Post:**

**Announcement Posts**
- Beautiful cover image
- Event details in caption
- Link in bio to booking page
- Hashtags relevant to your location and theme

**Sample/Example Photos**
- Past work from similar sessions
- Shows the quality clients can expect
- Include testimonials in caption
- "This could be you!" energy

**Behind-the-Scenes Content**
- Location scouting photos
- Prop shopping or setup
- Your excitement and personality
- Makes it feel accessible and fun

**Countdown Posts**
- "3 weeks until Fall Minis!"
- "5 spots left!"
- "Sold out in 48 hours!"
- Creates urgency

**Client Feature Posts**
- Repost sneak peeks from past mini sessions
- Get permission and tag the client
- Shows social proof (real people love your work)

**Stories Strategy:**

- Post event announcement to stories + save to Highlights
- Share countdown stickers ("Only 2 weeks left!")
- Poll stickers: "Who's booking Fall Minis?" with Yes/Already Booked options
- Question stickers: "What questions do you have about mini sessions?"
- Link sticker directly to booking page (if you have 10k+ followers)
- Repost client stories who mention their excitement

**Hashtag Strategy:**

Use a mix of broad and local hashtags:

**Broad hashtags:**
- #FallMiniSessions
- #FamilyPhotographer
- #MinisessionEvent
- #FallFamilyPhotos
- #HolidayCardPhotos

**Local hashtags:**
- #PortlandPhotographer (your city)
- #PDXFamilies (your city abbreviation)
- #PortlandMoms (your city + audience)
- #OakGroveParks (your location)

**Branded hashtag:**
- #YourBusinessNameMinis (create a branded hashtag clients can use)

#### Facebook Strategy

**Where to Post:**

**Your Business Page:**
- Event announcement with booking link
- Updates as spots fill
- Sample photos
- Client testimonials

**Your Personal Profile:**
- Share from your business page
- Personal invitation to friends/family
- More casual, behind-the-scenes content

**Local Community Groups:**
- "Moms of [Your City]"
- "[Your City] Local Events"
- "[Your City] Buy/Sell/Trade" (if allowed)
- "Photographers in [Your City]" (to connect with non-competing photographers)

:::warning Group Rules
Always check group rules before posting! Some groups don't allow business promotion. If you're not sure, message the admin first.
:::

**Facebook Event:**

Create a Facebook Event for your mini sessions:
- Makes it easy for people to invite friends
- Sends automatic reminders to people who RSVP
- Shows social proof ("12 people are interested")
- Centralizes all event info in one place

**How to create:**
1. Go to Events on your business page
2. Create Event
3. Add cover photo, details, and booking link
4. Invite your followers
5. Post updates to the event as slots fill

**Boosting Posts (Paid Advertising):**

Consider spending $20-50 to "boost" your announcement post:
- Targets people in your area
- Reaches people who don't follow you yet
- Can narrow by demographics (parents, ages 25-45, etc.)
- Often worth it for filling those last few spots

### Website Marketing

Don't forget your own website!

#### Add a Banner or Announcement

At the top of your homepage:
- Eye-catching banner
- Brief details
- Clear "Book Now" button

**Example banner text:**
> 🍂 Fall Mini Sessions Now Booking! Limited spots available - Book your session today →

#### Create a Dedicated Landing Page

Some photographers create a full landing page:
- Event details
- Sample photos
- FAQ section
- Booking form or link
- Testimonials from past mini session clients

This page serves as:
- Destination for social media traffic
- SEO-friendly content (people searching "fall mini sessions Portland")
- Comprehensive info hub

#### Blog Post About the Event

Write a blog post announcing the event:
- "Fall Mini Sessions 2024: Everything You Need to Know"
- Include all details, what to wear, location info, booking link
- Helps with SEO
- Shareable content
- Can be updated annually

### Referral and Word-of-Mouth Marketing

Your best marketing often comes from happy clients spreading the word.

#### Ask Past Clients to Share

**Personal message:**

> Hi Sarah,
>
> I'm running Fall Mini Sessions next month and would love your help spreading the word!
>
> If you know any families who might be interested, would you mind sharing the booking link with them? I'd be so grateful!
>
> [Booking link]
>
> And if anyone you refer books a session, I'll give you $25 off your next session as a thank you. 💛

#### Referral Incentive Program

Offer a reward for referrals:
- $25 off next session for referrer
- $25 off for person being referred
- Everyone wins!

**How it works:**
1. Create unique referral codes for past clients (SARAH25, MIKE25, etc.)
2. Track which code was used at booking
3. Credit both parties after the referred client attends their session

#### Social Sharing Encouragement

Make it easy for people to share:

**Create shareable graphics:**
- Quote the post: "I'm so excited for @yourhandle's Fall Mini Sessions!"
- Template graphic clients can repost to stories
- Pre-written caption they can copy/paste

**Example shareable story template:**
> Can't wait for @yourhandle's Fall Mini Sessions! 🍂 If you're local and want beautiful family photos for holiday cards, check out [link]! Only a few spots left!

### Creating Urgency

Urgency drives bookings! Here's how to create it ethically:

#### Limited Capacity

**This is real urgency, not fake!**

- "Only 12 spots available"
- "Limited to one day only"
- "This location is only perfect for 2 more weeks"

Don't artificially limit if you could easily add more dates/slots. But if 12 sessions is genuinely your max, say so!

#### Early Bird Pricing

Rewards people who book immediately:

- First 5 bookings get $25 off
- Book by September 15th for $225 (regular price $250)
- Early bird code expires in 48 hours

**Why it works:** People don't want to miss out on a deal, so they book NOW instead of "thinking about it."

#### Countdown to Price Increase

- "Price increases September 15th"
- "Book by Friday to lock in $250 rate"
- "After this week, price goes up to $275"

**Real example:**
> ⏰ Price increases Friday! Right now mini sessions are $250, but on September 15th the price goes up to $275. Book this week to lock in the lower rate!

#### Show Spots Filling

**Visual urgency:**

- "8 out of 12 spots booked!"
- "Only 4 morning spots left"
- "Afternoon sessions sold out, mornings still available"

**Why it works:** Social proof + scarcity. If others are booking, it must be good. And if spots are disappearing, I need to act now!

#### Time-Limited Offers

- "48-hour flash sale - $50 off!"
- "Weekend only: Book Saturday or Sunday and save"
- "This code expires Monday at midnight"

**Why it works:** Clear deadline forces decision-making.

### Leveraging Social Proof

People want to see that others have booked and loved the experience.

#### Testimonials

**Where to use them:**
- Social media captions
- Email announcements
- Booking page
- Website landing page

**Example:**

> "Sarah was amazing! The mini session was quick and stress-free, and we got the most beautiful family photos for our holiday cards. Worth every penny!" - Emily R.

#### Client Photos (with Permission)

**Share past mini session results:**
- "This is what you can expect!"
- "Sarah's family loved their Fall Minis last year"
- Shows real results from real clients

**Always get permission first:**
> Hi Sarah, I'd love to share one of your mini session photos when I announce this year's Fall Minis. Is that okay with you?

#### "Fully Booked" Badge

**If you sell out, shout it out!**

- "Fall Mini Sessions SOLD OUT in 3 days!"
- "Thank you to everyone who booked!"
- "Join the waitlist for next event"

**Why it works:** Proves demand. Next time you announce, people will remember it sold out and book faster.

### Paid Advertising (Optional)

If you have a marketing budget, paid ads can fill those last few spots or reach new audiences.

#### Facebook/Instagram Ads

**When to use:**
- Event is 2-3 weeks away and you have open spots
- You're new to the area and building your client base
- You want to reach beyond your current followers

**Budget:**
- Start with $20-50 for a week-long campaign
- Target local area (10-20 mile radius)
- Target demographics (parents, ages 25-50, etc.)

**Ad Format:**
- Carousel ad with 3-5 sample photos
- Clear headline: "Fall Mini Sessions - Only 5 Spots Left!"
- Strong call-to-action: "Book Your Session Today"
- Direct link to booking page

**Results to expect:**
- Reach: 2,000-5,000 local people
- Engagement: 50-200 post interactions
- Bookings: 1-5 new bookings (depending on offer and targeting)

#### Google Ads (Advanced)

**When to use:**
- You're in a competitive market
- You want to appear when people search "family photographer near me"
- You have a larger marketing budget ($100-300)

**Targeting:**
- Keywords like "fall family photos Portland"
- "Mini session photographer near me"
- "Holiday card photographer [your city]"

**Landing page:**
- Dedicated landing page on your website
- Clear booking CTA
- Mobile-optimized

### Common Marketing Mistakes

**Mistake 1: Announcing Too Late**

Announcing 2 weeks before the event doesn't give people time to plan or save money.

**Fix:** Announce 6-8 weeks in advance. Give people time to book, pay, and prepare.

**Mistake 2: Only Posting Once**

You post the announcement once and expect it to sell out. Most people need to see it 3-5 times before booking.

**Fix:** Post weekly updates with different angles (sample photos, location sneak peeks, urgency updates).

**Mistake 3: No Call-to-Action**

Your post says "Fall Minis coming soon!" but doesn't say where to book or include a link.

**Fix:** Every post should have a clear CTA: "Book your spot here: [link]" or "Link in bio!"

**Mistake 4: Burying the Booking Link**

Booking link is on page 6 of your website, or in an Instagram story that expired 3 days ago.

**Fix:** Pin the booking link in your Instagram bio. Create a Story Highlight called "Book Minis" that stays visible.

**Mistake 5: Ignoring Email**

You only post on social media and assume everyone sees it. Algorithm shows your post to ~10% of followers.

**Fix:** Always email your list! Email has much higher open/engagement rates than social media.

**Mistake 6: Generic, Boring Copy**

"Fall mini sessions available. Book now." - this doesn't excite anyone.

**Fix:** Tell a story, show beautiful photos, explain the value, create urgency. Make people WANT to book.

**Mistake 7: Not Following Up**

Someone comments "Interested!" on your post and you don't reply or message them.

**Fix:** Reply to every comment and DM interested people with the booking link and details.

**Mistake 8: Inconsistent Branding**

Every post looks different - different colors, fonts, styles. People don't recognize it's you.

**Fix:** Create templates in Canva with consistent colors, fonts, and layouts. Your posts should be instantly recognizable.
