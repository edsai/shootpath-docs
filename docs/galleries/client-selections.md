# Client Selections and Favorites

## Quick Reference

Client selections let your clients mark their favorite photos by clicking a heart icon. This helps you understand which images resonate most and creates a personalized selection list for each client!

**How Client Selections Work:**

- Client browses gallery
- Clicks heart icon on favorite photos
- Selections are saved automatically
- You can view their selections from the job page
- Use selections for albums, prints, or thank-you cards

**What Clients Can Do:**

- **Favorite unlimited photos** (or set a limit)
- **View all their selections** in one list
- **Download just their favorites**
- **Unfavorite** to change their mind
- **Share favorites** with family for input

**How You Use Selections:**

- See which photos clients love most
- Build wedding albums with their favorite 50 shots
- Create canvas prints of top selections
- Understand what style resonates for future shoots
- Offer upsells based on their preferences

**Setting Up Selection Limits:**

- Unlimited (default) - Client can favorite as many as they want
- Custom limit (e.g., "Select your top 50 for the album")
- Minimum requirement ("Must select at least 20")

**Next Steps:** Learn about [gallery settings](./gallery-settings) to control selection limits and download options, or explore [sharing galleries](./sharing-galleries) to deliver selections to clients.

## Detailed Guide

### What Are Client Selections?

When clients view their gallery, they can mark photos as favorites by clicking the heart icon. These favorited photos become their "selections" - a personalized list of images they love most.

**Why selections matter:**

**You learn what they love** - Which photos make them say "wow"? Selections tell you!

**Album creation** - "Select your favorite 50 photos for your wedding album" gives you exactly what to include.

**Client involvement** - Clients feel part of the process and get exactly what they want.

**Upsell opportunities** - "I see you loved these 10 family portraits - want a canvas gallery wall?"

**Style insights** - Track which types of photos get selected most. Candids? Posed? Details? This informs your shooting and editing style!

**Gift selection** - Parents choosing photos for thank-you cards or grandparents picking prints can favorite their choices.

### How Selections Work for Clients

From the client's perspective, selections are simple:

#### Browsing the Gallery

Client opens the gallery link you sent. They see all photos in a beautiful grid layout.

#### Marking Favorites

When they see a photo they love:
1. Hover over the photo (desktop) or tap it (mobile)
2. Click/tap the heart icon
3. Heart fills in, photo is added to favorites
4. Counter updates: "12 favorites"

**It's that easy!** No complicated process, just one click.

#### Viewing All Selections

At any time, client can:
- Click "View Favorites" or "My Selections"
- See all their favorited photos in one view
- Download just their favorites
- Unfavorite photos to change their mind

#### Changing Their Mind

Made a mistake? Favorited too many? Client can:
- Click the heart again to unfavorite
- Selections update immediately
- No limit on changes (unless you set a selection limit)

### How Selections Work for You

As the photographer, you have full visibility into client selections:

#### View Client Selections

From the job page:
1. Go to Files & Gallery tab
2. Look for "Client Selections" section
3. See all photos the client favorited
4. View as a grid or list

**You see:**
- Which photos were selected
- How many total selections
- When selections were made
- Which photos were selected most (if multiple clients/family members)

#### Download Selected Photos

Need to pull just the client's favorites?
1. Go to client selections view
2. Click "Download Selections"
3. Get a ZIP file of just their favorited photos

**Why this is useful:**
- Send favorites to print lab for album or canvas
- Share with album designer
- Create a "Favorites" subfolder in your archives
- Easier than manually finding 50 photos out of 400!

#### Use Selections for Products

Common workflows:

**Wedding albums:**
"Select your favorite 50 photos for your album" → Client favorites 50 → You download selections and design album with those images.

**Canvas prints:**
"Which family portrait do you want on canvas?" → Client favorites 1-2 → You send those to canvas printer.

**Thank-you cards:**
"Pick your favorite photo for thank-you cards" → Client favorites 1 → You order cards with that image.

**Parent albums:**
"Have your parents select their 20 favorites" → Parents browse and favorite → You create parent albums with their choices.

### Setting Selection Limits

You can control how many photos clients can select:

#### Unlimited Selections (Default)

No limit - client can favorite as many photos as they want.

**Best for:**
- General browsing and sharing
- When you're delivering all digital files anyway
- Clients who just want to mark favorites for personal use

#### Custom Selection Limit

Set a maximum: "Select up to 50 photos for your album"

**How it works:**
- Client favorites photos
- Counter shows "12 of 50 selected"
- At 50 selections, they must unfavorite before adding more

**Best for:**
- Album-focused packages where you need a specific count
- Print packages with limited selections
- Helping clients narrow down choices (decision paralysis prevention!)

#### Minimum Selection Requirement

Require clients to select a minimum: "Please select at least 20 favorites"

**How it works:**
- Client must favorite at least 20 photos
- Cannot submit selections until minimum is met
- Counter shows "12 selected (minimum 20 required)"

**Best for:**
- Album packages where you need enough content
- Print bundles that include X prints
- Ensuring client engagement with the gallery

#### Range Requirements

Combine minimum and maximum: "Select 40-60 photos for your album"

**How it works:**
- Client must select at least 40
- Cannot select more than 60
- Counter shows "45 selected (40-60 required)"

**Best for:**
- Album designs with specific page count requirements
- Packages with defined scope
- Preventing too few or too many selections

### Configuring Selection Settings

To set selection limits:

1. Open the job
2. Go to Files & Gallery tab
3. Go to gallery settings
4. Find "Client Selections" section
5. Choose limit type:
   - Unlimited
   - Maximum of X photos
   - Minimum of X photos
   - Between X and Y photos
6. Save settings

Limits take effect immediately for client view!

:::tip Communicate Expectations
Always tell clients the selection requirement in your instructions: "Browse your gallery and select your favorite 50 photos for the album. Once you've made your selections, let me know and I'll start designing!"
:::

### Selection Workflows by Job Type

Different photography genres use selections differently:

#### Wedding Photography

**Album-focused approach:**

You deliver: 400 edited photos in the gallery

You ask: "Select your favorite 50-60 photos for your 30-page album"

Client: Favorites 55 photos over 2-3 days

You: Download selections, design album with those images

**Why this works:** Client gets exactly the album they want with photos that matter most to them!

**Print package approach:**

You deliver: 400 photos

Package includes: 20 prints of their choice

You ask: "Select 20 favorites for your print collection"

Client: Favorites 20

You: Send those 20 to print lab

#### Portrait Photography

**Simple approach:**

You deliver: 30 edited photos

You ask: "Feel free to favorite your top picks! Great for sharing with family."

Client: Favorites 8-10 they love most

You: Note which style/poses they prefer for future sessions

**Upsell approach:**

You deliver: 30 photos, package includes 10 digital files

You ask: "Select your 10 favorites to download. Additional photos available for $25 each."

Client: Favorites 10 (or more if buying extras)

You: Deliver selected 10, invoice for extras if applicable

#### Event Photography

**Client use approach:**

You deliver: 200 event photos

You ask: "Browse and favorite your top shots! Perfect for marketing materials."

Client: Favorites 15-20 they'll use for social media and website

You: Get insights into which shots resonated for future events

#### Family Photography

**Multi-stakeholder approach:**

You deliver: 40 family photos

You ask: "Have everyone in the family browse and favorite their picks! I want to see which ones the whole family loves."

Family: Mom favorites 12, Dad favorites 8, grandma favorites 6

You: See which 3-4 photos everyone favorited (those are the winners!)

### Best Practices for Client Selections

**Explain the purpose clearly** - Don't just say "favorite photos." Explain WHY: "Select 50 favorites for your album" or "Mark your top picks for prints."

**Set realistic limits** - Don't ask clients to narrow 400 photos down to 5. That's painful! 40-60 selections for an album is reasonable.

**Give them time** - Selection takes thought. Allow 5-7 days for clients to browse, show family, and decide.

**Follow up** - If selections are needed for album production, remind client after a few days: "No rush, but just wanted to check if you've had a chance to select your album favorites!"

**Use selections to reduce back-and-forth** - Instead of "Which photos do you want?" → "Browse the gallery and favorite your picks, then I'll design the album!"

**Don't rely solely on selections** - Some clients never select favorites (procrastination, indecision, life gets busy). Have a backup plan: "If I don't hear from you in 2 weeks, I'll design the album with my favorite 50 shots."

**Thank them for selections** - "Thanks for selecting your album favorites! I'll get started on the design this week."

**Show examples** - "Here's what your album will look like with your 50 selections" helps clients visualize the outcome.

### Common Questions

**Can clients change their selections?**
Yes, anytime! They can favorite and unfavorite photos until you tell them selections are locked.

**What if client doesn't make selections?**
Follow up with gentle reminders. If they don't respond after 2 weeks, you can either: (1) design the album with your favorites, or (2) extend the deadline.

**Can multiple people select from the same gallery?**
Yes! Share the gallery link with family members. You'll see who favorited which photos.

**Do selections affect what's in the gallery?**
No! Selections are just markers. All photos remain in the gallery regardless of selection status.

**Can I see selections before client is done?**
Yes, you can view selections in real-time as client makes them.

**What if client selects more than the limit?**
If you set a maximum of 50, they can't select a 51st photo until they unfavorite one.

**Can clients download just their selections?**
Yes! There's a "Download Selections" button in their gallery view.

**Do selections expire?**
No, but you can lock selections after a deadline: "Please make your selections by March 15th."

### Troubleshooting Selection Issues

**Client says they can't favorite photos:**
- Check that selections are enabled in gallery settings
- Try different browser
- Check if they hit selection limit

**Selection count doesn't match:**
- Client may have unfavorited some
- Multiple family members may be selecting
- Refresh the page

**Client accidentally favorited wrong photos:**
- They can unfavorite and reselect
- Or you can clear all selections and have them start over

**Gallery has thousands of photos, selections overwhelming:**
- Use collections to organize into smaller groups
- "Select 10 from Ceremony, 20 from Portraits, 15 from Reception"
- Makes decision-making easier!
