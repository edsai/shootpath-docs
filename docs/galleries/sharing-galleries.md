# Sharing and Delivering Galleries

## Quick Reference

Sharing your gallery is the final step - getting that gallery link into your client's hands so they can view, download, and enjoy their photos!

**How to Share a Gallery:**

1. Finish uploading photos and organizing collections
2. Configure settings (downloads, watermarks, expiration)
3. Preview gallery to confirm everything looks perfect
4. Copy the gallery link
5. Send link to client via email or through ShootPath
6. Client clicks link and views gallery

**Automatic Notifications:**

ShootPath can automatically email clients when:
- Gallery is published and ready
- New photos are added
- New collections are published
- Gallery will expire soon (reminder to download)

**Manual Sharing Methods:**

- **Email through ShootPath** - Built-in delivery email
- **Copy and paste link** - Send via your own email
- **Client portal** - Client logs in and sees gallery
- **Text message** - Send link via SMS
- **Social media** - Share private link via DM

**What Clients Receive:**

Clients get:
- Gallery link (unique URL)
- Instructions on viewing and downloading
- Expiration date (if applicable)
- Password (if gallery is password-protected)

**Mobile-Friendly:**

All galleries work beautifully on phones and tablets! Clients can browse and download on any device.

**Next Steps:** After sharing, track client engagement and mark the job complete when photos are delivered successfully!

## Detailed Guide

### When to Share Your Gallery

Share your gallery after:
- All photos uploaded (or the collection you're sharing)
- Collections organized (if using them)
- Settings configured correctly (downloads, watermarks, expiration)
- Preview looks perfect
- You're ready for client to view

**Timing strategies:**

**Sneak peeks (24-48 hours after shoot):**
Upload 5-10 favorites → Share immediately → Build excitement

**Highlights (1-2 weeks after shoot):**
Upload 50-75 favorites → Share early → Keep client engaged while you finish editing

**Full gallery (3-4 weeks after shoot):**
Upload all photos → Share complete delivery → Client has everything

**Choose timing that fits your workflow and client expectations!**

### Automatic Gallery Notifications

The easiest way to share galleries - let ShootPath notify clients automatically!

#### Enabling Auto-Notifications

Go to: Gallery Settings > Notifications

**Enable:**
- "Send notification when gallery is published"
- "Send notification when new photos are added"
- "Send notification when new collections are published"
- "Send expiration reminder (7 days before)"

**How it works:**

1. You upload photos and publish gallery/collection
2. ShootPath automatically sends email to client
3. Email includes gallery link and instructions
4. Client clicks and views immediately

**No manual email needed!** ShootPath handles delivery automatically.

#### Customizing Notification Emails

You can customize the email clients receive:

**Subject line:**
- Default: "Your Gallery is Ready!"
- Custom: "Your Wedding Photos are Here! 🎉"

**Email body:**
- Add personal message
- Include viewing instructions
- Mention expiration date
- Provide support contact

**Branding:**
- Your logo in email header
- Brand colors
- Professional signature

**Configure in:** Settings > Email Templates > Gallery Notification

:::tip Personal Touch
Even with auto-notifications enabled, send a quick personal text or call to let VIP clients know their gallery is ready. That extra touch shows you care!
:::

### Manual Sharing Methods

Prefer to send gallery links yourself? Here are the options:

#### Method 1: Email Through ShootPath

**From the job page:**
1. Go to Files & Gallery tab
2. Click **"Share Gallery"**
3. Opens pre-filled email with gallery link
4. Add personal message
5. Click **"Send"**

**Email includes:**
- Subject: "Your Gallery from \{Your Business Name\}"
- Gallery link (clickable)
- Photo count
- Instructions for viewing/downloading
- Expiration date
- Your signature

**Best for:** Professional delivery email with gallery link embedded!

#### Method 2: Copy Gallery Link

**From the job page:**
1. Go to Files & Gallery tab
2. Click **"Copy Gallery Link"**
3. Link copied to clipboard
4. Paste into your own email, text, or message

**Gallery link format:**
```
https://yourshootpath.com/gallery/abc123xyz456
```

Long, unique, secure!

**Best for:**
- Sending via your own email client (Gmail, Outlook)
- Text messages
- Direct messages on social media
- When you want full control over messaging

#### Method 3: Client Portal Access

**How it works:**
- Client logs into their ShootPath portal
- Sees all their jobs
- Clicks "View Gallery" on relevant job
- Gallery opens automatically

**No link needed!** Client accesses through their account.

**Best for:**
- Clients who are already using the portal (contract signing, payments)
- Multiple touchpoints (quote, contract, gallery all in one place)
- Professional, organized experience

**To enable:** Gallery is automatically linked to job. No extra setup needed!

#### Method 4: QR Code

**Generate QR code for gallery:**
Gallery Settings > Sharing > Generate QR Code

**Client scans with phone camera → Gallery opens!**

**Best for:**
- In-person delivery
- Printed materials (thank-you cards, packaging)
- Events (post QR at venue)

**Unique use case:** Include QR code in wedding album or print packaging. Client can revisit digital gallery anytime!

```

to this email!

Thank you again for trusting me to capture your special day. It was an
honor to be part of it!

Best,
[Your Name]
[Your Business Name]
[Contact Info]
```

### Sharing Sneak Peeks vs Full Gallery

Different strategies for staged delivery:

#### Sneak Peek Delivery (Day 2)

**Email subject:** "Sneak Peek from Your Session! 👀"

**Message tone:** Excited, teasing, short

**Example:**
```
I couldn't wait to share a few favorites!

Here are 5 sneak peeks from yesterday's session:
[Gallery Link]

The full gallery (30 photos) will be ready in 2 weeks.
These should hold you over until then! 😊
```

**Why this works:** Builds anticipation for full gallery while delivering instant gratification!

#### Highlights Delivery (Week 2)

**Email subject:** "Your Highlights Are Ready! 🌟"

**Message tone:** Warm, showcasing your favorites

**Example:**
```
I've been editing your photos and had to share my favorites!

Your highlights collection (50 photos) is now live:
[Gallery Link]

These are MY top picks from your session. I'm working on the
remaining photos and will have the full gallery ready next week!
```

**Why this works:** Gives clients something to enjoy while you finish editing everything!

#### Full Gallery Delivery (Week 3-4)

**Email subject:** "Your Complete Gallery is Here! 🎉"

**Message tone:** Celebratory, comprehensive

**Example:**
```
Your full gallery is now live!

All 425 edited photos from your wedding day:
[Gallery Link]

Organized into collections:
- Getting Ready (50 photos)
- Ceremony (75 photos)
- Portraits (100 photos)
- Reception (200 photos)

Download everything, favorite your picks, and share with family!

Your gallery will be available for 60 days (until March 15).
Please download all photos before then.

Thank you for trusting me to capture your incredible day!
```

**Why this works:** Complete, professional delivery with clear instructions!

### Password-Protected Gallery Sharing

If gallery has password protection enabled:

**Email must include:**
1. Gallery link
2. Password (sent separately or in same email)
3. Instructions for entering password

**Example email:**
```
Your private gallery is ready!

Gallery link: [URL]
Password: weddingday2026

To access:
1. Click the gallery link
2. Enter the password when prompted
3. Browse and download your photos

The gallery will be available for 90 days.
```

**Security tip:** For very sensitive content (boudoir, confidential events), send link via email and password via text message. Two-factor sharing!

### Social Sharing by Clients

If social sharing is enabled, clients can share the gallery:

#### How Clients Share

From gallery view:
- Click "Share" button
- Copy link
- Share on Facebook, Instagram, email, etc.

**What happens:**
- Recipient clicks link
- Views gallery (if sharing is allowed)
- Sees your watermarked photos

#### Controlling Social Sharing

**Enable sharing for maximum exposure:**
Gallery Settings > Sharing > "Allow clients to share gallery"

**Great for:**
- Weddings (family wants to see!)
- Events (attendees share with each other)
- Marketing (more people see your work)

**Disable sharing for privacy:**
Gallery Settings > Sharing > "Restrict sharing (client only)"

**Best for:**
- Sensitive content
- Client requests privacy
- Corporate/confidential work

### Tracking Gallery Engagement

After sharing, you can see if/when client viewed the gallery:

#### Access Analytics

Gallery Settings > Access Log

**You can see:**
- Date/time client viewed gallery
- How many times viewed
- Which collections browsed
- What was downloaded
- IP address (general location)

**Why this matters:**

**Confirm delivery** - "Client viewed gallery 2 hours after I sent link. Success!"

**Follow up** - "Haven't viewed in 3 days. Should I check if they got the email?"

**Troubleshooting** - "Client says they can't access gallery. Log shows no views. Probably wrong email or spam folder."

**Engagement insights** - "Client viewed ceremony collection 5 times but didn't view reception. Interesting!"

Best practices for post-delivery communication:

#### Check In After 24 Hours

**If client viewed:** Great! Let them enjoy.

### Common Sharing Issues & Solutions

**Client says they didn't receive gallery link:**

Check:
- Email address correct?
- Check their spam/junk folder
- Resend link manually
- Try different email address
- Send via text message instead

**Client can't open gallery:**

Check:
- Link copied completely (no truncation)
- Gallery hasn't expired
- Password required? Did you provide it?
- Try different browser
- Try incognito/private mode

**Client says downloads aren't working:**

Check:
- Are downloads enabled in settings?
- Has selection requirement been met?
- Gallery expired?
- Internet connection stable?
- Try different browser

**Gallery link looks weird/broken:**

Check:
- Link copied completely
- No spaces or line breaks in URL
- Send as clickable link, not plain text
- Use link shortener if needed (bit.ly, etc.)

### Best Practices for Gallery Sharing

**Test before sending** - Preview gallery as client. Make sure everything works perfectly before sharing!

**Communicate expiration clearly** - "Your gallery will be available for 60 days - please download all photos by March 15."

**Include instructions** - Don't assume clients know how to use galleries. Include brief how-to instructions!

**Personalize delivery** - Add personal note referencing something specific from their session. Shows you care!

**Follow up** - Check in 24 hours later if they haven't viewed. People miss emails!

**Remind before expiration** - 7-day warning prevents last-minute panic downloads.

**Enable auto-notifications** - Less manual work for you, instant delivery for clients!

**Use watermarks strategically** - Watermark previews so shares on social media include your branding.

**Mobile-test** - Many clients will view on phones. Make sure mobile experience is smooth!

**Celebrate delivery** - This is a big moment for clients! Match their excitement: "I'm so excited to share your photos!" 🎉

### After Delivery: Marking Complete

Once gallery is shared and client has downloaded photos:

1. Confirm final payment received
2. Gallery successfully delivered
3. Client is happy
4. Mark job complete in ShootPath

**To mark complete:**
- Open job
- Go to workflow tab
- Check "Gallery Delivered"
- Click "Mark Job Complete"

**What happens:**
- Job moves from "Active" to "Complete"
- Gallery remains accessible (until expiration)
- Stats updated (jobs completed this year)
- Ready for your next project!
